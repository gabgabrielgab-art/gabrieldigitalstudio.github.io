FAQ

Questions small business owners ask first.

I don’t have a logo yet. Is that a problem?

No. The Presence package includes a logo refresh, and I can design a simple mark from scratch if you’re starting fresh. Spark clients can add a logo for a fixed £450.

Do I have to write all the text myself?

You give me the facts in a short questionnaire and our call. I shape that into clear, friendly copy and send it to you for approval.

I already have a website. Do we start over?

Not always. If your current platform is sound, I redesign on top of it. If it’s slow or hard to edit, I’ll explain why a rebuild costs less in the long run.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. You get a simple editor for text, photos, hours and prices, plus a short recorded walkthrough so you never feel stuck.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and every login and design file is handed over at launch, whether or not you join the care plan.

How do I get more Google reviews?

I set up a short review link and QR code you can put on receipts, the counter or follow-up messages. Asking at the right moment does most of the work.

How much does a small business website cost in London?

Most of my clients spend between £795 for a one-page site and £2,450 for a full rebuild with branding and local SEO. You get a fixed quote after our free call, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take to build a website?

About four weeks from our first call to launch. A one-page Spark site can be live in as little as two weeks if your content is ready.

Do you only work with businesses in London?

I’m based in London and happy to meet in person anywhere in the city. I also work remotely with small businesses across the UK, with calls, previews and sign-off all online.
